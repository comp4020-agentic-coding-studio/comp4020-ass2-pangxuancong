The twelve weeks are not ordered by difficulty. They follow the sequence of
decisions the game actually forces: what you are handed, what you may change
about it, what you buy, how you fight, what you do when the plan stops working,
and finally whether the mode that produced all of it is well designed.

Each week states a claim rather than a topic — a proposition a reasonable
person could argue with — and names which of the mode's four removals it is
about. Week 1 carries the lecture slides. For dates and due dates on one page,
see [the schedule](/schedule/).
