Three pieces of work and nothing else: no quiz, no participation mark, no
attendance component. Each is an account of decisions made inside a game state
you did not choose, and each is due after the last week it rests on has been
taught.

All three are marked on the same four criteria, weighted the same way —
**quality of reasoning under uncertainty 40, identification of the real
trade-off 30, use of evidence 20, clarity of the account 10**. The weighting is
itself the argument: most of the mark is for the thinking, and none of it is
for the result. A defended decision that lost the game is not penalised for
losing.

Submission, late penalties, extensions, the use of generative tools and the
rule about which patch you argue from are on the [policies
page](/policies/).
