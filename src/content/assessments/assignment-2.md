You will be issued one frozen mid-game state. Where Assignment 1 asked what
you would do with a hand, this asks what you can read off a game already in
progress.

## What you receive

A single position, roughly two thirds of the way through a game:

- both team compositions, with every augment taken
- current items on all ten champions
- health and mana as they stand
- which ultimates are available and which are not
- the gold each player is holding but has not spent
- the position of the wave and of the ten champions on the bridge

You are one named champion in this position. The state is what you have; there
is no additional information available on request, because there would not be
in a game either.

## What you submit

Roughly 1,800 words, in five parts.

**Composition.** What functions does each team cover, over-cover, and lack?
Name the gap on both sides. Do not list champions and describe them — that is
not a reading.

**Win condition.** One sentence for your team, one for theirs. Both must
describe how the next two fights are actually won, not a general aspiration.
"We scale" is not a win condition. "Their only route through our front is the
one ultimate they are currently holding" is.

**Space.** Where should the five of you be standing, in what order, and what
is the enemy's shortest path to the person at the back? Argue from the
position you were given, including the wave, the bushes and the range bands.

**Engagement.** Under what condition does this fight become worth taking, and
which of the regenerating variables are you waiting on? If the answer is that
you cannot wait, say what is forcing you and price the fight anyway.

**Adaptation.** What in your current build or plan has been invalidated by
something in this state, and how do you know? At least one thing has. Point at
the observation, not at general principle.

One of your claims must be tested against actual data — match history or a
patch note, sourced and patch-labelled. One is enough. The point is that you
can tell which of your claims is the kind that data could settle, and which
are arguments that no dataset will decide for you.

## How it is marked

The same four criteria as every other task in this course, with reasoning
under uncertainty carrying the most weight.

The failure mode this assignment is built to catch is the essay that is
correct in general and unconnected to the state in front of it. Everything you
write should be false of some other game. If a paragraph would survive being
pasted into a submission about a completely different position, it is not
analysis — it is recital, and it will be marked as such.

## Practical

Submitted through the course page by the deadline. Cite the patch the state is
drawn from, which is named in the materials, not the patch that is live when
you write.
