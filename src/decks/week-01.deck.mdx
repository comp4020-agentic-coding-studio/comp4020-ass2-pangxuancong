## What this mode takes away

- `no-pick` — you do not choose your champion
- `no-recall` — you cannot retreat and reset
- `one-lane` — there is no map to be elsewhere on
- `forced-contact` — you cannot decline the fight

These four are the spine of the semester.

```notes
Every week names at least one of these. Tell them that now — it makes the
schedule legible as an argument rather than a list of topics.
```

## Where the strategy went

On the Rift:

**decide what you want** → **draft a champion that does it**

Here:

**receive a champion** → **work out what it can be made to do**

The order reverses. Nothing is removed.

```notes
Emphasise that the second is harder, not easier. You do not get to choose the
problem, which is the definition of most real decision-making.
```

---

## Three things that get confused

1. **receiving** a strong option — luck
2. **recognising** a strong option — knowledge, and lookupable
3. **recognising a normally strong option is wrong here** — skill

```notes
Pause on the third. Ask which of the three the win-rate table can help with.
Answer: the second, and only the second.
```

## The strongest objection

> Randomness lowers the skill ceiling.

Over one game, the better player wins less reliably here than on the Rift.

**This is true.**

```notes
Concede it properly. A course that will not state the case against itself is a
guide with a syllabus attached. Do not rush this slide.
```

---

## But it measures the wrong thing

Lower correlation between skill and **outcome**

≠

lower ceiling on **decision quality**

The objection assumes decisions are graded by whether they won.

```notes
This is also how this course grades. Flag now that a defensible choice which
lost the game earns full marks in all three assessments, because they will not
believe it the first time.
```

---

## Where the semester goes

The twelve weeks follow the order the game forces:

`roll` → `reroll` → `augment` → `build` → `composition` →
`space` → `fight` → `die` → `adapt` → `fall behind` →
**`question the system`**

```notes
Not beginner to advanced. The decision sequence. Week 12 turns the same
analysis onto Riot's design of the mode, which is why it is last rather than a
revision week.
```
